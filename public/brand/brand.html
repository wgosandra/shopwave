Concept 02 · Crest · Approved

One continuous swell draws the W of Wave, with a second swell trailing behind it for depth. Everything below is generated from that single path, so the lockups, the icons and the social image can never drift apart.

How the mark is built

Five anchor points, symmetric about the centre. The tangent is horizontal at both valleys and at the centre peak, which is what keeps the shape reading as a wave rather than a drawn letter W. The trailing swell is the same path, offset down and to the right.

Lockups

The horizontal lockup is the default. Reach for the stacked one only where the horizontal will not fit above its minimum size, and the symbol alone only where the name is already on screen.

Clear space and minimum size

The clear-space unit is x, the height of the lowercase o in the wordmark. Keep x clear on all four sides, and x between the symbol and the wordmark. Nothing else sits inside that box.

Horizontal lockup140 px wide on screen, 32 mm in print. Below that the wordmark's arcs start to close up.
Stacked lockup96 px wide, 24 mm in print.
Symbol alone16 px. At 24 px and below use favicon.svg, which carries the optical corrections.
Optical correctionsThe favicon thickens both swells and shortens the trailing offset, so the two stay separate at 16 px instead of blending into one blob.

Colour

Two brand colours and nothing else. The neutrals are warm rather than the blue-grey most UI kits ship with, so they sit with the orange instead of fighting it. All of it is in tokens.css, ready to drop into the Tailwind theme.

What not to do

Each of these is a real render of the logo, not a description of one.

Don'tStretch. Scale both axes together, always.
Don'tRotate. The swell has a horizon. Keep it level.
Don'tAdd effects. No shadows, bevels or gradients.
Don'tRecolour. Graphite and orange, or a mono variant.
Don'tSink it into the ground. Use the reverse lockup instead.
Don'tCrowd it. Nothing enters the clear space.
