Brand exploration · Round 1

Four marks for ShopWave

Four independent directions, each a symbol plus the ShopWave wordmark, drawn as hand-written SVG with no more than three colours. Every one is shown on white and on ink, at presentation size and at the sizes that actually decide a logo: 32px, the browser favicon, and 16px, the tab strip. Pick one and I will build it out into the full asset set.

All four share one wordmark, a monoline geometric alphabet built only from full circles, circle arcs and straight lines on a single grid: x-height 20, cap height 28, one stroke weight per concept. So the choice in front of you is really a choice of symbol, and whichever one wins, the lettering is already consistent with it.
